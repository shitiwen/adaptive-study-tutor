# Continuity and course state

Use visible, editable state only. Prefer the long-term subject branch, a project file, or a user-provided
record. A temporary lesson chat is the classroom workspace; the long-term branch is the course ledger.
Do not claim that a new chat has silently inherited the old chat's full history.

## When to write a card

Produce a detailed card proactively at the end of a chapter, a major knowledge unit, or a temporary lesson
chat. Also produce one before handing off after a context warning, a long derivation, a change of textbook,
or a change of teacher emphasis. Do not interrupt every short exchange with a card.

## Detailed state-card schema

Use this structure. Write `未提供` or `尚未验证` instead of guessing.

```text
【课程交接状态卡｜日期】

一、课程定位
- 课程/长期分支：
- 教材、版本、章节、页码或PPT页：
- 学校当前进度：
- 本阶段目标：
- 老师明确强调/考试范围：

二、课堂边界与符号
- 本次实际讲到：
- 已确认的符号、定义、约定、坐标/电流方向：
- 教材与补充资料存在的顺序或记号差异：
- 本次未讲但已准备的缓冲内容：

三、知识结构
- 已讲知识点及依赖关系：
- 已解释但尚未检查：
- 已检查但尚未确认迁移：
- 已确认掌握（附证据）：
- 薄弱点：
- 阻塞点及缺失前置知识：

四、学习证据
- 用户能独立完成的动作：
- 用户出现过的典型错误及错误原因：
- 已使用过的例题/变式，避免下次机械重复：
- 尚未完成的检查题：

五、教学设计交接
- 下次开场应先回顾：
- 下次第一步：
- 建议的讲解表示方式（公式/图/表/电路图/代码/类比）：
- 下次应避免的解释方式：
- 下次合适的暂停或提问点：

六、下一步
- 下一节课目标：
- 首道回忆检查：
- 主讲内容：
- 备用内容：
- 暂不进入的内容：
```

## Evidence rules

- `已确认掌握` requires an observable explanation, derivation, application, or successful transfer;
  “看起来听懂”只能写入“已解释但尚未检查”。
- Record the first wrong step and the reason it was tempting, not just “做错了”。
- Separate school-exam scope from enrichment. A clear supplementary explanation must not silently become
  a teacher requirement.
- Preserve exact notation and sign conventions when they affect later work.
- Do not copy the full transcript or every score. Keep only information that changes the next lesson.

## Handoff workflow

### PREP → temporary lesson chat

Pass the unit order, source map, prerequisite assumptions, visuals, examples, checks, likely errors,
stopping point, and unused buffer. If the learner says “先不要开讲”, stop after this handoff.

### Temporary lesson chat → long-term subject branch

At the end, generate the detailed card above. The learner may paste it into the long-term branch or save it
as the course's visible progress file. The long-term branch should retain the newest card and may keep dated
older cards as history.

### Long-term subject branch → next temporary lesson chat

Ask the long-term branch for a “下一节课启动卡”. It should compress the latest detailed card into:

- one-sentence current position;
- one prerequisite check;
- the next teaching objective;
- notation and teacher-scope constraints;
- the first prepared activity;
- what must not be assumed.

Paste that start card into the new lesson chat, together with only the relevant textbook pages or slides.

### MASTERY → normal teaching

Return the evidence-based status, exact remaining gap, next review date or trigger, and the smallest next
task. Do not leave the entire course in strict mastery mode after one difficult concept.
